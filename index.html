About

Building technology that
solves meaningful problems.

I'm Poojith — a Forward Deployment Engineer at HighRadius, debut author of The Non-Stop Rollercoaster, stage anchor, and someone who can't sit still long enough to stay in one city.

My work lives at the crossroads of data, people, and clarity. Whether I'm transforming client data in the O2C cycle, writing about emotions that young adults are too overwhelmed to name, or holding 25,000 people through a starnight — I'm always trying to make something complicated feel simple.

I studied Computer Science at KIIT University, Bhubaneswar, and now call Hyderabad home.
